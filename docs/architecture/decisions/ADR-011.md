# ADR-011 — Tactical intelligence: forcing-line shot detection and evidence-based criticality

- Status: **Accepted** (G9, 2026-11-20)

## Context

G9 must find tactics and critical positions, and it must work for **Brazilian**, where the only
engine is the platform's weak internal engine (G8). Draughts tactics are dominated by
**forced captures**: a sacrifice obliges the opponent to capture, which can set up a larger
capture in return ("shot"/combination). Mandatory capture makes such lines narrow enough to
enumerate exactly with the Rules Core.

## Decision

1. **Forcing-line detector** (`tactics.forcing`). From the side to move S:
   - opponent nodes continue only while the opponent **must capture**;
   - S may play captures, or quiet moves that **force** an opponent capture (at most
     `max_quiet` of them, default 2);
   - S may stand pat when not obliged to capture;
   - material (man 1, king 3) is counted when forcing stops;
   - a *shot* is a first move whose forced line gains at least 1 man.

   It is exact within its rules (Rules Core moves) but **blind beyond the end of the forced
   line**.
2. **Threats** are the opponent's captures in a null-move view (side to move flipped).
3. **Criticality** (`tactics.critical`) is a set of independent, explained flags:
   - `shot`, only when a real choice exists;
   - `threat` (≥ 2 pieces);
   - `only move` (internal engine full-width move values, gap ≥ 1 man, depth 6);
   - `volatile` (a final best move that none of the previous 2 depths chose, or a score
     swing ≥ 1 man);
   - optional `divergence` from G7/G8.

   A single legal move is never a decision.
4. **Thresholds are parameters** with provisional defaults. Every report carries them and its
   evidence (lines, gains, move values).
5. **Validation by external judges.** Detector claims are measured against Scan and Kingsrow on
   International positions (the G9 report). Brazilian claims are reported with that measured
   International precision as the best available proxy.

## Consequences

- Fast (≈ 1–2 ms per position for shots) and explainable: each claim comes with its line.
- Known false positives (≈ 5–10% of sacrifices costing ≥ 1 man by the judges' measure) come
  from counter-play after the forced line. Combining the detector with search (the internal
  engine's move values are already in the report) mitigates this; a deeper verification step
  is future work.
- `volatile` is the noisiest flag (it depends on the internal engine's depth). Its threshold
  should be revisited when the engine is stronger (G14).
